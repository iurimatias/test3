How to play

Camera

  • Arrow keys or move the mouse to a screen edge — scroll
  • Middle-drag or Space + drag — pan
  • Mouse wheel — zoom · Click the minimap — jump

Selecting

  • Left-click a unit · drag a box for many
  • Double-click — every unit of that type on screen
  • Shift+click — add to selection
  • Ctrl+1…9 set a group · 1…9 recall it

Commanding

  • Right-click — move, gather, build, repair or attack
  • A then click — attack-move · S — stop
  • B — build menu (with a villager selected)
  • Right-click with a barracks selected — set its rally point

Shortcuts

  • H — jump to your Town Center
  • . — cycle idle villagers · F — select your army
  • Space — jump to the last attack · P — pause
  • F2 — toggle 2× game speed

Winning

Gather food, wood, gold and stone. Build houses for population, train an army, advance through four ages, and destroy every red building and unit. A villager carries 12 at a time and then walks it home, so a nearby drop-off means far less walking — put a Lumber Camp next to the trees and a Mill next to the berries. When the berries run out, build farms.

Victory!
